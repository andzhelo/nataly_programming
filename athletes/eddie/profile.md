## Weaknesses and how Nataly wants them handled

- **RMU: legs fly too high and he chicken-wings.** Drills: low-ring transitions with
  both elbows turning over together (reset if one leads); kip swing + hip pop with legs
  together, "kick down and back, knees below hips"; paused ring dips 3 s at bottom.
  Practice format she chose (Nov): 1 toes-to-rings + 1 RMU × 2, 5 sets. If he
  chicken-wings or legs fly, next minute drops to 1 clean rep.
- **HSW: cannot walk on hands.** Give drills and attempts towards the wall, not free
  HSW. Her HSW drill circuit (Mon Nov 9, 2026), keep reusing:
  1. Kick Up to Leg Touch, 4×4, rest 1:00–1:30. Hebrew cue: compression, push hard
     from the hands, lower to the floor under control.
  2. Jumping Toe Taps, 4×4, rest 1:00–1:30. Video: https://youtu.be/JhkxeI2-Ecc?t=558
     (from 9:08). Like the plate drill but without the plate, moving forward.
  3. Handstand Walk From the Wall, 5 attempts, rest 1:00. Face the wall, shift weight
     forward without pushing off the legs, take a few steps.
  4. Handstand Walk towards Wall, 5 attempts, rest 1:00.
  Also used: chest-to-wall HS hold every 2:00 × 5, 20 s, protraction focus.
- **Recovery:** Oct 4 note said arms were fried after RMU + weighted pull-ups + metcon
  in one day. Keep big pulling volume away from pulling metcons.
