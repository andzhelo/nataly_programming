## Sun 08 Nov

### A. Warm up
Warm up (~10 min)

250 m Row

8 False Grip Ring Rows
10 Air Squats
30 Single Unders + 20 Double Unders

Then: Back Squat empty bar 2 x 5

### B. RMU Drills
- Low Rings Hip Pop  + Snap Back Pull + Transition: 3 x 3
  Focus: BOTH elbows turn over at the same time (fixes the chicken wing).
  Pull the rings to the bottom of the ribs, then sit up fast over the rings.
  If one elbow goes first - stop, reset, redo the rep.
  rest 1:00
- Muscle Up Drills: 3 x 5
  Kip swing + hip pop on rings - LEGS TOGETHER
  Kick DOWN and back, not up. Knees stay below the hips, toes stay slightly in front.
  The power comes from the hips to the rings, not from the knees.
  Film one set from the side.
  rest 1:00

### C. Ring Muscle-Up
- Kipping Ring Muscle-Up: 5 x 2
  5 sets of:
  (1 Toes to Rings + 1 RMU) x 2

  The toes to rings teaches the kip timing - kick down from the toes-to-rings straight into the muscle-up swing.
  Legs together, both elbows turn over together.
  rest 1:30-2:00

### D. Strength
- Back Squat: 1 x 1
  Build to a HEAVY SINGLE for today - NOT a failure attempt.
  Stop when the next jump would be a grinder (RPE 9 max).
  Suggested jumps from 90 kg: 100 - 105 - 110 - 115...
  rest 2:30-3:00 between heavy singles
  Log the top weight.
- Back Squat: 2 x 5
  2 sets of 5 @ 75% of today's top single
  rest 2:30
  Log the weight.
