## Thu 28 Aug

### A. Clean and Jerk Complex
Squat Clean + Hang Clean + Split Jerk
4x(1+1+2) @ 75-80%// RPE 8-9
NOTES:
· Rest at least 2:00 between sets.
· For today, it is okay to hang on to the bar for all reps. Be sure to take your time with each part of the complex, making sure your grip and stance are where they need to be with the core braced before initiating each rep.
· For the split jerks, reset the bar at the shoulders completely, avoiding any "touch-and-go" reps at the shoulders.
· Remember, the weight ranges do not mean that you need to be at the upper end of this range. Earn your weights and stay lower in the range if needed in order to execute quality reps. Focus on movement quality and developing confidence with the barbell in these sets.
