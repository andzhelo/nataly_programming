Bench Press:

· Use a spotter if you are approaching failure. If you don't have a spotter, stay a touch below a true 5RM for today.

### C. Metcon
AMRAP 15:
30/25 Cal Fan Bike
25 Burpee to 6" Target
NOTES:
· This is a simple AMRAP, but if you push yourself hard, it should not feel easy. Try to find the hardest sustainable pace possible.
· Aim to start slower than the hardest pace you can hold for 10:00 on the fan bike and try to hold the same pace for the entire AMRAP.
· This can be a lot of burpees at a time, so be sure to pace your movement if necessary by stepping up or stepping down from each rep.
· Once you are finished, take notes over the average pace you held on the fan bike (wattage & RPM's/ cadence), the technique you used on the burpees, and how your initial pace changed, if it did, as you went through this AMRAP.

### E. Chest to Bar Skill Work
Chest to Bar Skill Work:
OPTION A:
· Accumulate 80 Reps in sets of 5-10
NOTES:
· If you max unbroken set of chest-to-bar would be twenty or more reps unbroken, choose this option. If not, then move to Option B.
· Pick a number that you can maintain for the entirety of the 80 reps, and rest as minimally as possible between sets. Ideally, each set will be the same number of reps, with possibly a small set for what's left at the end.
· Even if your max set of unbroken reps is much higher than 5-10 reps, this type of practice is important for metcon settings where you may be presented with a lot of chest-to-bar at once.
· This will be the highest volume of reps we go to in this format!
OPTION B:
3 x :15-30 Chin Over Bar Hold; 1:00 Rests
3 x :30 Butterfly Kip Drill Practice; 1:00 Rests
3 × 1:00 Max Rep Chest-to-Bar In Sets of 3-5,
or 1:00 Chest to Bar Practice; 1:00 Rests
NOTES:
· If your max unbroken set of chest-to-bar would be less than twenty reps, choose this option.
· If you can't perform a chin-over-bar hold, perform a foot-assisted chest-to-bar hold.

### C. Front Squat
EMOM 9:
1-8: 1 Front Squat @80% // RPE 8-9
9: 7 Front Squat @ 75% // RPE 8-9
NOTES:
· Perform one rep at 74.7kg on the minute for eight minutes, then on the ninth minute, perform a set of seven front squats at 68.4kg.
· You will feel fatigued before the set of seven, but the weight should still allow for well-executed reps for the entire set. Use the RPE recommendations to scale back if needed after warming-up.
· This is the last week of this EMOM before we change gears next week.

### D. Metcon
For Time:
1000m Row
60 GHD Sit-Up
40 Alternating Pistol
20 Cluster @60/45kg
Time Cap: 12 Minutes
NOTES:
· This metcon starts to compound on itself with the fatigue that will set in the deeper you get. Find a pace that pushes you hard from the start but still allows you to always transition straight into the next movement.
· If you have not had much exposure to the GHD sit-up, consider breaking these reps up or even scaling the amount of reps, range of motion, or both. This can be a potent movement, especially for those without much experience on the GHD.
· If you don't have pistols, scale to either box step-ups or air squats for double the reps.
· For the clusters, the weight should be moderate.
You might be able to cycle this load when fresh or for the last few reps, but in order to stay moving, quick singles will likely be the fastest approach for the majority of the reps.
