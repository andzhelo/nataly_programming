3x(1+1+1+1)  @ 80% // RPE 8.5
NOTES:
· Rest 1:30-2:00 between sets.- Reset at the ground between the first split jerk and the second squat clean.- These doubles are meant to be heavy, but this shouldnt be a max-out. Go off the RPE guide if needed to ensure that rep quality is maintained.
B) Squat Clean + Split Jerk
2x(1+1) @85% // RPE 8.5
NOTES:
· Rest 1:30-2:00 between sets.- Be sure you can be consistent with these singles.
Take your time after the squat clean before the split jerk, being sure your grip and stance are where you want them and your core is braced.
