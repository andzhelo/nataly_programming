## Sun 09 Feb — Back Squat

### A. Back Squat
AMRAP 15:
20 Bar Facing Burpee
15 Chest to Bar
10 Clean & Jerk @ 135/95lbs | 60/45kg
NOTES:
· Find a steady pace on this AMRAP to keep your rounds very consistent. For most people, the burpees should be the place to regulate the pace; going faster if you are feeling good and slowing the movement down if needed to regulate the heart rate and maintain output.- Be sure you can finish the chest-to-bar in three sets or less consistently. If not, then scale back the rep count or scale the movement to pull-ups or jumping pull-ups.- The clean & jerk load should allow for quick singles.
If not, then scale back the weight on the bar.
