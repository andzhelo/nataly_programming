### B. Tempo Pause Front Squat
Tempo Pause Front Squat
3x3 @ 12x1 Tempo / RPE 7
Rest 2:00-3:00 Between Sets.
NOTES:
· "12x1" means that you will take :01 to descend, :02 at the bottom, stand fast, and :01 at the top to reset between reps.
· This is a deload week. Build to a weight to use for all three sets that is moderate, but you could still do about three more reps with if you wanted to on each set.
· Keep the pause at full depth. Stand straight up from the bottom of each rep with no bounce.
