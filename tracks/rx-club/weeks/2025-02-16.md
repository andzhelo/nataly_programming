## Sun 16 Feb

### A. Skill
A) 5-10:00 Practice:
Pirouettes OR Handstand Walk
NOTES:
· If you are proficient with a handstand walk, then set out a 25ft course with a mark at 12.5ft. Aim to add in a pirouette at this mark. Once you get comfortable here, add in 1-2 more pirouettes into the 25ft. Rest at least :30 between all attempts.
· If you are not very proficient with a handstand walk, then try to first practice by accumulating 5-10ft unbroken sections. Be sure to rest at least :30 between attempts.
· If handstand walking is very difficult for you, practice wall walks or handstand walks around a box with the toes set on top of a box.
