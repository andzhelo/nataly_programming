## Sun 16 Feb — METCON

### A. METCON
Amrap 1212 Toes to Bar
12 Wall Balls @ 20/14lbs
12/9 Calorie Fan Bike
NOTES:
· Pick a pace you can maintain for unbroken sets on the wall balls and hard bikes.- If you need to break up the toes-to-bar, that is okay, but if you will be breaking more than twice per set, scale down the movement or the number of reps.- Focus on the transitions between movements. The faster you can make these, usually, the better your score will be!

## Tue 18 Feb — Metcon

### A. Metcon
For Time:
21 Snatch @ 95/65lb | 42,5/30kg
20 Box Jump Over w/ Step Down @ 24/20"
15 Snatch @ 115/75lb |52,5/35kg
20 Box Jump Over w/ Step Down
9 Snatch @ 135/95lb | 60/42,5kg
20 Box Jump Over w/ Step Down
Time Cap: 12 Minutes
NOTES:
· The snatches can either be done as power snatches or squat snatches- Be careful not to go too big on the first set when the bar is light. Most athletes will need to break these reps up from the start to manage the fatigue of this workout, even opting for singles if they can be cycled quickly. Take note of how far back you step away from the bar between reps/sets if you do move.- On the box jump overs, aim to find a steady cadence to move from the start. For the last 20 reps,try to pick up the pace here!
