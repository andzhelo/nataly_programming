### B. Skill
EMOM 10:
1: 15/12 Cal Fan Bike
2: 50 Crossover Single Under (Cap @ :40)
NOTES:
· Hit the fan bike at a pace to get your heart rate up, but not at an all-out pace. The emphasis of this AMRAP should be on the crossovers. Cap the fan bike at :50 each time.
· Take this as an opportunity to work on crossover single unders under fatigue. After the initial cross, count each rep as every time the rope passes under your feet.
· If you don't have crossover single-unders, move to :30 of practice each time, or simply to 5-10:00 of practice instead of this EMOM.
