## Sun 07 Apr

### A. Week Notes
This is the transition week between the Open and the qualifiers. Great job everybody during the Open, you can be proud of yourself! You’ve earned a little de-load before another push next week! So, here is what we suggest:
Do not go into the dark zone this week.
Focus on moving well.
Take the time to do a reflection and ask yourself what quarterfinals movements you need to work on the most. Try to gain some confidence by practicing technical details like the way down the rope climb, your ability to breathe on the GHD Sit-ups, Jerk and OH Squat technique…
There will be some competition simulation workouts on Saturday and Sunday. Make sure you use this as a practice run, don’t get too caught up in your performance on this, but you want to expose yourself to a small dose of competition intensity.
Take as much rest as needed between the Mock QF workouts.
