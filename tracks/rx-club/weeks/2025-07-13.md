### A. Snatch Complex
High Hang Squat Snatch + Squat Snatch + Overhead
Squat w/ :03 Descent
4 x (1+1+1) @ 65% - 73% // RPE 7
NOTES:
· Rest at least 1:30 between sets.
· This week, we are lowering the weight for a deload.
