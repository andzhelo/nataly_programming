## Sun 17 Mar

### A. Week Overview
This is the transition week between the Open and the Quarter Finals.
Main goal: Supercompensation
This is a full on deload week, where we focus on speed with lower total volume and intensity. The main goal for this week is to allow your body to acclimatize to the best state it’s been all year. You’ve put in the training, you must have confidence in your current ability and allow nature to do it’s thing this week.
If you’ve qualified for the quarterfinals, then use the next few days to recover and focus on technical movements that may happen in the quarterfinals. If you did not qualify, then use this week to have fun without focusing on anything in particular.
You will see some workouts over Friday and Saturday where the goal is: Competition simulation, this is meant to stimulate your energy and muscular systems to perform for the QF weekend, not this weekend. It will require an RPE 9, save the edge for the competition that matters.
So, here is a recommended approach to the week:
Do not go in the dark zone, Most sessions should not exceed an RPE 7
Focus on moving well and breathing. Putting together sound movement mechanics that flow with your breath.
Carve out some time before ‘weakness sessions’ to visualize your movement beforehand.
Practicing technically demanding, small details like the way down the rope climb, your ability to breath on the GHD Sit-ups, Foot placement on burpee over whatever’s, Jerk and OH Squat technique…ect..

### B. Next two days
Over the next two days the sessions are slightly dialed back in the amount of total workouts in order to allow us to bring in some intensity in some of the workouts. This is to prime your system, but not to destroy you. Remember we will do a ramp up starting next week. The goal is to be ready to throw down your best 100% effort over 2 workouts per day, not multiple sessions per day at 80%.
-When you see the goal as “Competition stimulation” this is when you need to give a 90% effort, leave a little bit in the tank but don’t be afraid to lie on the floor afterwards.
