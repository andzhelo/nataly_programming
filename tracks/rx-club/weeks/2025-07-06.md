## Mon 07 Jul

### A. Deadlift
4 Sets:
6 Deadlift @ 72 % // RPE 86 Straight Leg Box Jump
NOTES:
· Rest at least 2:00 between sets.- Keep control if you choose to hang on to the bar for touch-and-go reps. Avoid bouncing the bar to gain momentum.- Move straight from the deadlifts into the box jumps.
Use any box height that will allow you to complete each rep with a straight-legged landing.- Step down from the box on each rep.

### C. Fan Bike Intervals.
