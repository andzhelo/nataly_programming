## Sun 06 Oct

### A. Handstand Push-Ups
Handstand Push-Ups
ーーーー
4 Sets:
1:00 On
· Max Reps Strict Handstand Push-Ups
· Dumbbell Strict Press @ 2x50/2x35lb
· Dual Dumbbell Overhead Hold @ 2x35/25lb
Rest 2:00 Between Sets.
NOTES:
· Scale for up to 10 box pike handstand push-ups for the strict handstand push-ups, if needed.
· For the dumbbell strict press, scale to a weight that will allow you to get at least five reps with your best technique, if needed.
· Flow: you will perform max reps strict handstand push-ups (or box pike handstand push-ups), and then move on to dumbbell strict press for max reps, and then hold the dumbbells overhead until the minute expires.
· "Max reps" for today means when you reach technical failure you are done with that movement.
Move on the the next exercise when your form breaks down.
· You might NOT make it through all three movements in one minute.
· Always begin with the handstand push-ups.

### B. Tempo Pause Front Squat
Tempo Pause Front Squat
2x3 @  RPE 8
1x2 @  RPE 8-9
2x1 @ RPE 9
Rest 2-3 Between Sets.
NOTES:
· "12x1" means that you will take :01 to descend, :02 at the bottom, stand fast, and :01 at the top to reset between reps.
· Keep the pause at full depth. Stand straight up from the bottom of each rep with no bounce.
· The weight ranges are only a suggestion, because the percentage of 1RM that can be pause squatted will vary from athlete to athlete. Defer to the RPE guide if needed, and feel free to go to a weight outside of the recommended range if this better fits the RPE suggestion.
