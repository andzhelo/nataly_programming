## Sun 14 May

### A. Week Overview
This week kicks off the second wave cycle of the base strength and endurance phase. You will notice a bigger distinction in the max effort to volume days as we use different lifts. For instance the back squat max effort % and front squat volume % is a bigger distinction than when we used the opposite in the previous wave cycle. Same goes for the bench press and strict press. We now use the bench for the max effort and strict press for the volume. Dynamic days will still remain similar and the endurance training will get longer.
Have fun :)
