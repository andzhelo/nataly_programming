For Time:
21-15-9-6-3
Handstand Push-Up
Front Squat @70/47.5kg
Bar Facing Burpee
NOTES:
· Starting the week off with a bang with a metcon paying homage to a CrossFit Regionals event from
2014. This piece is all about managing the first two rounds in order to go unbroken and fast on the burpees for the last few rounds.

· This workout is a spin-off of "The Standard" from the
