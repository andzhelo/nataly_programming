### B. Tempo Pause Front Squat
Tempo Pause Front Squat
· Build a heavy single @ 12x1 Tempo / RPE 10
NOTES:
· "12x1" means that you will take :01 to descend, :02 at the bottom, stand fast, and :01 at the top to reset between reps.
· While the goal is to go for a heavy single, this should be the absolute heaviest single you can perform without any deviation to your technique. Avoid bouncing out of the bottom after the pause to stand, and fight to keep proper body positioning throughout the rep. If your technique starts to deviate significantly from your baseline as you add load, then cut this session off.
· As a suggestion for your build-up, start around Ado your Personal Record below for 3-5 reps, then add weight in 10-20lb / 5-10kg increments while decreasing the rep count to singles as you build. Do this until you hit your max for today.
