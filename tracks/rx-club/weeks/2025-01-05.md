NOTES:
· Perform max reps of a kipping handstand push-up in each :20 interval, picking an option from the above list that will allow you to work for the majority of the EMOM without rest.
· Moving to an easier option is okay if your rep speed or rep quality decreases in this EMOM.

### C. Handstand Push-Ups
DROPSETS.
3 Sets of 1:00 Work...
· Strict Handstand Push-Up
· Box Pike Handstand Push-Up
· Dumbbell Strict Press @ RPE 7
· Dumbbell Strict Press @ -15%
Rest 2:00 Between Sets.
NOTES:
· Each set should be 1:00 of total work. Start at the top of the list, and move to the next movement only if your rep speed or your rep quality decreases, or if you hit failure. It is okay if you do not go through every movement on the list before the minute is up.
· If you don't have strict handstand push-ups, start with box pike strict handstand push-ups
· For the dumbbell strict press, pick a weight you can use for 6-10 unbroken reps, and move to a pair of dumbbells that is 15% lighter if you have time remaining.
