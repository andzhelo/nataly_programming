Squat Snatch + Overhead Squat
2x(1+1+1+1) @ 85-90% // RPE 9
1-3 Sets @ (1+1+1+1) @ Weight of Choice
NOTES:
· Rest at least 2:00 between sets.
· Drop the bar and reset at the ground after the low hang squat snatch. Do not go with a touch-and-go rep for your last squat snatch.
· Today is the day to push this complex to a heavy weight, but try to stay away from failure. Use the 1-3 extra sets to move to a weight that will challenge you, but you are still confident you can move well with. If your first two sets go poorly, drop the weight and hit the one set at a lower weight than your first two sets, in an effort to clean up your movement.
