## Sun 02 Feb — METCON

### A. METCON
5 Rounds for time
24 Wall Balls
12 Power Cleans @ 60/42,5 kg
6 Bar Muscle ups
NOTES:
· Be sure your medicine ball weight will allow you to get each set of 24 reps done in two sets or less when fresh. If not, then scale back the number of reps.- On the power cleans, you should be able to cycle the weight you use for 12 unbroken reps when fresh.
However, if you decide to break these reps up each round for strategic reasons, that is okay.- If you dont have bar muscle-ups, scale to 10 C2B

## Mon 03 Feb

### A. METCON
5 rounds for Time
12 Dumbbell Deadlifts
9 Dumbbell Hang Power Cleans
6 Dumbbell Should to Overhead
3 Devils Presses@ 50/35 lbs x 2

### B. Intervals
5 Sets of AMRAP 3:
25/20 Cal Row
12 Toes to Bar
Max Cal Ski In Remaining Time
Rest 1:00 Between AMRAPS.
NOTES:
· Be sure you can finish the row under 1:40consistently. If not, then scale back the amount of calories.- The toes to bar should be able to be done in two quick sets or unbroken. If you will not be able to sustain this, move to hanging knee raises or V-ups.- Most athletes should plan to have a minimum of :30 on the ski. If after doing the first set, you find you are getting less time than this, scale the next sets back a touch.
