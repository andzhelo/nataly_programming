### G. "Flight Simulator"
"Flight Simulator"
5-10-15-20...45-50-45...15-10-5
Unbroken Double Unders *
* Rest as needed between sets.
Time Cap: 10 Minutes
NOTES:
· Be sure to respect the 10:00 time cap. The idea is that this will help manage volume and save the calves/ankles, especially if you are not yet very proficient with this movement.

### B. Power Clean + Power Jerk
Power Clean + Power Jerk
5x(2+2) @62-65% // RPE 7
NOTES:
· Rest at least 1:30 between sets.
· These should NOT be touch-and-go reps on the power cleans. Take the time to reset at the ground between reps and a moment of pause to brace/set the hands and feet before the power jerk.
· Before dipping down for the second jerk, take a moment to pause and make sure your core is braced and your grip and stance are where they should be.

### C. Deadlift
Deadlift
1x5 @65%  // RPE 7
1x3 @75% // RPE 7
3x1 @ 85-90%// RPE 8.5 - 9
NOTES:
· Rest at least 2:00 between sets.
· These should NOT be touch-and-go reps. Reset the bar at the ground each time, without dropping the bar from the top of each rep.
· For the last three sets, perform a single rep anywhere in the range. If you will have difficulty jumping straight to 93.5kg, perform 1-2 singles to build to this weight, counting these reps as sets toward your 3x1.

### D. Conditioning
EMOM 40:
1: 14/11 Cal Ski
2: 5/5 Nonalternating Pistol + 6-10 Chest to Bar
3: 16/14 Cal Standing Bike Erg @ Damper 10/8
4: 5 Strict Handstand Push-Up + 25ft Handstand Walk
5::40 D-Ball / Sandbag Bear Hug Hold @45/30kg
NOTES:
· This will be an eight-round EMOM, as opposed to the normal ten-round format.
· The cardio machines should always be completed between :40-:45. Adjust the calories as needed. If you don't have access to a Bike Erg, sub in a fan bike or any other cardio machine for :40-:45 worth of effort.
· Choose a number of chest-to-bar you can complete unbroken.
· The d-ball / sandbag hold should be completed unbroken each time. Adjust the weight if needed.
· For the remaining gymnastics skills, scale back where necessary to keep sets fast and unbroken.
For example: scale pistols to box step-ups, strict handstand push-ups to Z-Press, and handstand walks to wall walks or bear crawls. Check out the Substitutions and Scaling Guide in Circle if needed:
https://hwpo-training.circle.so/c/resources-842857/ substitutions-scaling
