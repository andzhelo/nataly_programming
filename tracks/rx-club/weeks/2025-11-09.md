### A. Fan Bike Intervals.
Fan Bike Intervals.6:00 @ 8 rpm slower than 10 min pace+Rest 3:00+6:00 @ 5  rpm slower than 10 min pace+Rest 3:00+6:00 @ 3  rpm slower than 10 min pace
NOTES:
· The idea here is to have three 6:00 efforts, each faster than the last. If you cant hold the prescribed paces exactly, or if you dont have a 10:00 test baseline, start at a moderately aggressive pace you can hold for the entire first 6:00 set. From there, build in pace across each of the next two sets.- Focus on getting the breath back under control during the 3:00 rest. This rest period should afford you just enough time to recover to come back faster each time.
