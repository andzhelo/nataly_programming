### B. 3-Position Snatch Deadlift
3-Position Snatch Deadlift:
5x2 w/ :02 Pauses @ +20lb / 10kg
NOTES:
· Rest for at least 1:00 between sets.
· Add 20lbs (or 10kg) from the weight of your top snatch single, and pause for :02 below the knee, above the knee, and just before extension at the top for each rep. Ensure the bar remains close to the legs at all times.
· Focus on keeping an upright torso and driving through the whole foot the entire way.
· If you have weightlifting straps, use them!
