### B. Tempo Pause Front Squat

5x2 @ Add your Personal Record below - Add your Personal Record below, 12x1 Tempo / RPE 8-9

· "12x1" means that you will take :01 to descend, :02 at the bottom, stand fast, and :01 at the top to reset between reps.

· The weight ranges are only a suggestion, because the percentage of 1RM that can be pause squatted will vary from athlete to athlete. Defer to the RPE guide if needed, aiming for a weight that is heavy, but could allow for one more rep on each set.
