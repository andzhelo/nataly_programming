## Sun 16 Mar — Skill

### A. Skill
EMOM 101: 200/180m Ski
2: 50 Crossover Single Under (Cap @ :40)NOTES:
· This is the same EMOM as two weeks ago, but with a bit more interference from the ski. Expect this to be a touch more challenging than if you did this with the fan bike before.- Take this as an opportunity to work on crossover single unders under fatigue. After the initial cross, count each rep as every time the rope passes under your feet.- Hit the ski at a pace to get your heart rate up, but not at an all-out pace. The emphasis of this AMRAP should be on the crossovers.- If you dont have crossover single-unders, move to :30 of practice each time, or simply to 5-10:00 of practice instead of this EMOM.

### A. Clean & Jerk

Halting Clean Deadlift + Floating Clean Pull + Floating
Squat Clean + Squat Clean + Power Jerk + Split Jerk 3x(1+1+1+1+1+1) @50-60%// RPE 5
Rest 1:00 Between Sets.
NOTES:
· Reset the bar on the ground after the floating squat clean.- Reset at the shoulders between jerks.----
B) Squat Clean + Floating Squat Clean + Split Jerk +Squat Clean:
5x(1+1+1+1) @ 70-75% // RPE 8
NOTES:
· Take a few build up sets to get to your listed weight, and then hit one complex at this weight. If needed, drop down from the weight listed in order to ensure quality movement and no misses.- You can hang on to the bar after the squat clean for the floating squat clean if you would like to, but drop the bar to reset at the ground after the split jerk.
C) Squat Clean + Split Jerk:
3x(2+1) @75% // RPE 8
Rest 1:30 Between Sets.
NOTES:
· Reset at the ground between squat cleans. Be sure to drive extra hard from the hips and legs on the split jerk, as they will be more fatigued than normal from the extra clean rep.- The goals for these two sets should be for consistency in your movement and no misses, even if there is some struggle with the weight. Go lighter to do this, if necessary.

15/12 Cal Fan Bike

NOTES:
