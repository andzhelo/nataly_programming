### E. Fan Bike Intervals
**The 60/60 Protocol:**
צירפתי הסבר על איך למדודו מהירות ממוצא ב ATHLETE NOTES
This test measures your ability to maintain output for a minute, a key indicator of fitness for short, intense work.
Warm-up: 5 minutes of easy spinning (50-60 RPM).
Ramp-up: 3 minutes at a moderate pace (65-75 RPM).
Rest:  2 minutes slow spin (50 RPM).
**The Test:**
Max Effort (60 seconds): Pedal as hard and fast as you can for one full minute. Push to your absolute limit.
Recovery (60 seconds): Immediately drop the pace to an extremely slow recovery spin (45-50 RPM).
Record:
Peak RPM: The highest instantaneous RPM achieved during the 60-second burst.
Average RPM: The overall average RPM achieved during the 60-second burst. (Use this number as your benchmark for the next 12 weeks).
Cool-down: 5 minutes of easy spinning.
