## Mon 22 Apr

### A. Week Overview
First of all, congratulations to those of you who competed up until this point. For most of us this marks the end of our season, we hope you can take some time to reflect on the wins and losses up until this point and learn from this past season. Take pride in your efforts and come back strong after a well deserved couple weeks of rebuilding.
To the rare ones who made it onto semi’s… wow, incredible! You need to hop on the elite track or email us: info@dekacomp.com to grab a coach to assist you moving forward. Congratulations!
This is a small two week rebuild phase in order to focus on full rest and recovery. This is VERY important in order to hit the reset button for you. This was a long competition season.
Each day is meant to be a notch above an active recovery day meant to stimulate multi-joint range, unilateral strength, and blood flow. These two weeks are an important layer mentally and physically. The athletes who do this week will hit the next phase: Conjugate Strength & Endurance with momentum and feeling ready to go!
Happy off season training!
