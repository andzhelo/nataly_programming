## Sun 18 Feb

### A. Reload Week
This week is our Deload week before the competition season kicks off. Your priority this week is recovery. That said, try and use the week to improve, work on cycle speed, refining technique, and hitting planned spikes of intensity when it is appropriate. A deload week is not a write off, it’s a week to grow and improve.
Further, if you are a perfectionist or overthinker, the approach of just a recovery week might be beneficial for you to give your mind a break and manage your stress.
