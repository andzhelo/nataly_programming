This is the first week of the final 3 week cycle of the base strength & endurance phase. This cycle we focus a little bit more on intensity with overload, so you will see end ROM movements at loads beyond your 1RM.
In the upper volume we switch to a pull rather than a push.
Everything else will be relatively consistent.
