3x3 @ 12x1 Tempo / RPE 7
Rest 2:00-3:00 Between Sets.

· This is a deload week. Build to a weight to use for all three sets that is moderate, but you could still do about three more reps if you wanted to on each set.
· Keep the pause at full depth. Stand straight up from the bottom of each rep with no bounce.
