### F. Row Intervals
Row Intervals

+
Rest 1:00
+
6-8x 100m @ Sprint, :30 Rests
ErgData Link: https://workout.ergdata.com/shared/x4ug2ss2
NOTES:
· Use the first 5:00 to settle into a smooth and sustainable pace, with a focus on technique. Once you get into the 100's, be sure to hold nothing back from the start. Start at an all-out sprint with the damper set 1-2 spots higher than you would normally row for a 2K effort. Try to sustain a sprint for all six sets, but if you have some drop-off in your output, that is okay with this format. Cut this off after six sets or seven sets if you are struggling to maintain anything close to a sprint pace.
· Even in the sprints, focus on maintaining proper row mechanics: legs first, then a lean of the torso to 11 o'clock, and finish through with the arms.
· The goal of this session is to improve your overall power on the machine and build in good habits for powerful starts.
