### C. Metcon
AMRAP 13
1-2-3-4-5-6...etc.
Rope Climb
300m Bike Erg
30 m D-Ball / Sandbag Bear Hug Carry @ 70/50
NOTES:
· For this AMRAP, only the rope climbs increase in reps. Start on round one with one rope climb, then two rope climbs on round two, then three on round three...etc. The bike erg and the d-ball / sandbag bear hug carry will stay the same on each round.
· This workout should feel like a slow grind. Pick a pace on the bike erg and sandbag carry that will allow you to move consistently through rope climbs.
· If you do not have access to a bike erg, sub in a 600m Row or Ski.
· The d-ball / sandbag bear hug carry does not need to be unbroken, but it should not feel heavier than an RPE 8.
