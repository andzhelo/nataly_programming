## Sun 11 May — "Flight Simulator"

### A. "Flight Simulator"
"Flight Simulator"
5-10-15-20...45-50-45...15-10-5
Unbroken Double Unders
Rest as needed between sets.
Time Cap: 10 Minutes
NOTES:
· Be sure to respect the 10:00 time cap. The idea is that this will help manage volume and save the calves/ankles, especially if you are not yet very proficient with this movement.- Each set must be unbroken. Once you complete a set, rest as much as you need to, then add five reps for another unbroken set. Do this until you get to 50 reps, then continue on in the same way, decreasing by five reps each time.- If you have been on the program for a while, compare when this was last programmed on March 25th, 2025 & April 15th, 2025.

## Tue 13 May

### A. Metcon
2 Rounds For Time:
40/32 Cal Fan Bike
30 Handstand Push-Up
800m Run
Time Cap: 24 Minutes
NOTES:
· Despite there being sets of 30 handstand push-ups in the middle of each round, this should be a very cardio-driven workout. Modify the handstand pushups to normal push-ups if this will allow you to keep moving.- Aim to begin moving at a hard, but controlled pace.
As you get into the last run, push hard to make the second 800m run faster than your first one.
