## Sun 21 Sep

### A. METCON
For Time:
200 DU 30 Burpee Box Jump Over @ 24/20"
20 D-Ball / Sandbag Clean @| 70/50 kg
10 Ring Muscle-Up
TC 12
NOTES:
· For most athletes, the crux of this metcon will be the d-ball / sandbag cleans, the muscle-ups, or both. Be sure to move through the run and the burpee box jump overs at a pace that allows you to preserve some energy for the back half of this metcon.- If you would struggle to cycle the d-ball / sandbag for quick singles, scale back to a lighter implement.- Move to burpee pull-ups if you dont have ring muscle-ups.- Take notes on how you feel for each portion of this metcon, how your transitions went, and where you think you could improve if you were to hit this metconagain.

## Thu 25 Sep

### A. Squat Clean + Split Jerk + Squat Clean + Split Jerk
Squat Clean + Split Jerk + Squat Clean + Split Jerk
3x(1+1+1+1) @75% // RPE 8
NOTES:
· Rest for at least 1:30 between sets.- Each set should be two clean & jerk singles. Be sure to reset at the ground between the first split jerk and the second squat clean: no touch-and-go reps!

### B. Strict Handstand Push-Up
A)  4 Sets:
50% max Strict Handstand Push-Up
NOTES:
· Rest 2:00 between sets.- These sets should each be exactly half of the amount of reps of your max unbroken set. Focus on rep quality and cycle speed!
B) 8 Sets:
2 Tempo Negatives - Strict Handstand Push-Up
Take a :05 tempo from top to bottom. You can choose to either go with a strict rep or kip to return to the top, or come off the wall and quickly restart from the top.
NOTES:
· Rest for at least 1:00 between sets.- If you dont have strict handstand push-ups, these reps can be a great way to develop to the requisite strength needed to acquire this skill. Move to negatives from a Box Pike Handstand Push-Up if needed.
