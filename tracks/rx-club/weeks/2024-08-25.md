## Sun 25 Aug

### A. Tempo Pause Front Squat
Tempo Pause Front Squat
3x3 @12x1 Tempo / RPE 8
NOTES:
· Rest 2-3 minutes between sets.
· '12x1"' means that you will take :01 to descend, :02 at the bottom, stand fast, and :01 at the top to reset between reps.
· Hit a weight that leaves about two reps in the tank. If the prescribed weight range is too high, then lower the weights to something more appropriate for you.
· Keep the pause at full depth. Stand straight up from the bottom of each rep with no bounce.
