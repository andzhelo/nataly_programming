## Mon 07 Apr — Metcon

### A. Metcon
2-2-2-3 Minutes Work / 1:00 Rest:
6/5 Strict Handstand Push-Up
15/12 Cal Fan Bike
In Remaining Time, Max Rep Power Snatch @ 60/45kg
NOTES:
· This piece will be 2:00 of working time for the first three sets, then 3:00 of working time for the fourth set. Always rest for 1:00 between sets.- If you cannot consistently complete 6/5 handstand push-ups unbroken, scale to either 8-12 Push-Up or 6-10 Dumbbell Z-Press.- The fan bike should be able to be completed under :50 each time, so scale back the calories or cut this off after :50 on the fan bike if needed.- The power snatch should be a weight that would allow for quick singles without more than :10 rest between reps. If you need to scale the weight, scale to 40/30 kg.

## Tue 08 Apr

### A. Overhead Squat

### B. Skill OPTIONAL
For Time:
15-12-9-6-3
Chest to Bar
Wall Ball @ 30/20lb; 14/9kg | 10/9ft
Time Cap: 6 Minutes
NOTES:
· The goal should be to aim for unbroken reps on both movements. If either movement is too aggressive for an unbroken first set, then scale back the difficulty of this piece.- Go to a lighter ball or lower target if needed to remain unbroken on the wall-balls.- Move to pull-ups if needed for the chest-to-bar.
OPTION
· If you dont have chest-to-bar or pull-ups to where you can consistently perform unbroken reps, consider moving to 5-10:00 of practice instead of doing this metcon!

### C. Metcon
3 Sets:
1000/800m Row
25 Box Jump / Step Down @ 24/20"
100 Double Under
Rest 2:30 Between Sets.
NOTES:
· Cap each set at 7:00.- Aim to hit the row ~02:05/500m and try to sustain this pace for each set. If you feel good after the first set, try to go faster on the next set. Keep a focus on your row technique, even if it means you are moving slightly slower for these sets.- The box height should allow you to cycle through 25 reps in ~1:00. Scale down the height of the box if needed.- The double-unders should never take you longer than 2:00. Cut down the number of reps or scale to Crossover Single Unders or Single Unders if needed.- Lastly, take notes on how well you maintained your pace across these sets, the effort you gave to hold these paces, and where/if you rested or broke up reps.
